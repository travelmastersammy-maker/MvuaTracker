Community Guidelines & Terms

Share genuine, recent rainfall observations. Do not post personal information, threats, harassment, vulgar or derogatory content, spam, or dangerous false claims. Comments may be screened or removed. Rain reports are unverified and are not emergency advice. Recent reports may be shown to all app users on the Kenya-wide map with coordinates rounded to about 1 km; the map shows place, observation time and severity, not comments. Reports are visible for up to 24 hours and retained for up to 30 days for moderation.
